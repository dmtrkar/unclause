How it works

Three steps to clarity

01

Upload your contract

Take a photo with your camera, import a PDF, or paste text directly. Unclause handles multi-page documents automatically.

02

Choose your role

Tell Unclause who you are — tenant, contractor, employer, buyer. The AI analyzes the contract strictly from your perspective.

03

Get your report

Receive a full risk report with a score, flagged clauses, plain-language explanations, and suggested rewrites ready to copy.

Features

Everything you need
before you sign

⚠️

Risk Score

Every contract gets an overall risk score with color-coded indicators. See at a glance whether a document is safe, risky, or somewhere in between.

🔍

Clause-by-Clause Analysis

Each risky clause is highlighted with a direct quote from the original text and a plain-language explanation of why it's problematic.

✏️

Ready-to-Use Rewrites

For every risky clause, Unclause suggests a better version. Old text crossed out in red, new text in green — copy it in one tap.

🌐

Language Version Comparison

Got a bilingual contract? Unclause detects all languages and checks whether the versions say the same thing — a common source of legal disputes.

📊

Compare Two Contracts

Select any two documents from your history. Unclause produces a side-by-side comparison of differences, risk changes, and recommendations.

📤

PDF Export

Export your full report as a PDF and share it with your lawyer, colleague, or client via any app on your device.
